Hive geometry & tipping calibration

Nominal opening 0.508 × 0.356 m; pivot 1.1165 m; stable tilt 30°. One simplified practice hive. The 185 g model threshold reproduces the official 8 Pollen or 3 Pollen + 3 Nectar calibration; other mixtures, impact dynamics and tipping duration are approximations. Changing these settings resets the individual-shot session.

How the model works & sources

Robot motion follows editable straight or cubic Bézier segments. Duration controls simulated travel time with a smooth ease-in/ease-out; it does not predict Pedro’s acceleration or tracking error. Shots fire while stationary after reaching each waypoint. Robot/frame collisions and game legality are not checked.

Flywheel I = count × shape factor × mass × radius². The speed-transfer model uses ωout = ωin / √(1 + mball × transfer² × radius² / I), then v = transfer × radius × ωout. Transfer is a speed ratio, not energy efficiency. Motor recovery is exponential toward the commanded RPM. Gravity is 9.80665 m/s²; air density is 1.225 kg/m³ with adjustable quadratic drag. Cd 0.47 is an estimate for testing. Ball spin, contact losses, bounce and internal basket dynamics are omitted.

Only a ball that clears the active opening from the outside is captured. The linked baskets switch sides at the calibrated load. During a tip, neither cell captures. Ball interactions with the frame and each other are omitted. No guarantee of real-world shot accuracy.
